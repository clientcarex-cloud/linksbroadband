What's the difference between Unlimited and FUP plans?

Unlimited plans give you the full speed with no data cap. High-Speed FUP plans offer much higher speeds (75–500 Mbps) up to a monthly data limit (600 GB – 3000 GB); after that, you continue browsing at a reduced post-FUP speed (3–15 Mbps) until your next cycle.

Are there installation charges?

Installation charges apply for monthly plans. Pay for 3 months in advance and installation is free. Pay for 6 months in advance and you get free router & installation.

What documents do I need?

1) Address Proof, 2) ID Proof and 3) a Passport Size Photo while submitting the application.

How do I pay my bill?

Packages are paid in advance. You can conveniently pay your bills through online mode.

Which areas do you serve?

How quickly can I get connected?

Once your documents and plan are confirmed and fiber is available in your building, our team schedules installation as quickly as possible — call us for the latest timeline in your area.
